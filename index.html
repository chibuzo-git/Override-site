You made the decision while calm. Then the urge changed the conversation.

A different job

Not another blocker. Not just a streak.

Porn blockers reduce access. Accountability apps add another person. Streak trackers show progress. Those tools can be useful, but they solve different problems. Override’s primary job is to interrupt the active urge with purposeful friction and a prepared reset. It can be used on its own or alongside longer-term blocking, support, or professional care.

Privacy by design

The app does not need to know who you are.

Override requires no account. Personal reasons, clean progress, commitments, selected apps, and intervention activity are stored locally on your device. Supported computer-vision processing happens on-device; camera content is not uploaded to Override servers.
