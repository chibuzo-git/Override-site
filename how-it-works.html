How Override works

An escape plan for the moment the urge starts negotiating.

Override turns an active porn urge into a guided physical reset. You start it yourself, selected apps are temporarily shielded, and a real-world action helps create distance from the automatic route.

The short answer

What happens when you tap Break the Urge?

Override immediately starts an intervention you prepared while calm. It shields your selected danger apps and gives you a concrete real-world action to complete. The experience is designed to move you out of passive scrolling and self-negotiation, then hold that distance through a short safety window.

The experience

Three shifts, one purpose: interrupt the automatic route.

1

Notice and start

You launch Break the Urge at the first sign that the familiar loop is taking over. Override does not claim to detect or eliminate an urge for you.

2

Create distance

The apps you selected during setup are temporarily shielded. That removes the quickest route while you move into an active reset.

3

Reset and return

A randomized real-world mission shifts your attention and physical context. When the intervention window ends, the shields expire automatically.

Why physical action

A reset should be harder to dismiss than another piece of advice.

When the urge is active, reading a reminder can leave you sitting in the same place with the same trigger. Override instead asks you to do something in the world around you. Supported on-device computer vision and motion capabilities help make the reset active without turning it into a detailed technical exercise.

The mission is not punishment and it does not promise to remove the urge. Its job is to create purposeful friction, change context, and give the immediate impulse time to lose momentum.

Temporary app shielding

What does Override block?

Override can temporarily shield the apps you choose during setup using the system controls available on supported Apple devices. It is not a permanent, internet-wide porn filter. Shielding begins during an intervention and ends automatically after the intervention window or after 15 minutes.

When a blocker may still help

If you want continuous website filtering or strict access restrictions across devices, a dedicated blocker may fit that job better. Override can complement that layer by giving you an active response after an urge begins. Compare blockers and urge interruption apps.

Set up before the urge

Make the important choices while thinking clearly.

Choose which apps should be shielded and save the personal reasons that matter to you. Override also includes a daily commitment and clean progress timer for the quieter parts of recovery. These support the core intervention; they are not substitutes for it.

Common questions

What buyers want to know before downloading

Does Override permanently block my apps?

No. Shielding is temporary and tied to the intervention window. Selected apps become available again automatically when that window ends or after 15 minutes.

Do I need an accountability partner or account?

No. Override requires neither an account nor another person. It is designed for someone who wants a private, self-started intervention.

Is my camera content uploaded?

No. Supported computer-vision processing happens on your device. Override does not upload your camera feed or scanned content to its servers.

What information stays on my device?

Personal reasons, commitments, clean progress, app selections, and intervention activity are stored locally, subject to your device settings and operating-system controls.

Is Override a treatment for compulsive sexual behavior?

No. Override is a self-help utility, not medical care or a treatment. If the behavior feels unsafe, unmanageable, or is causing serious distress, a qualified clinician or local crisis service can provide support beyond what an app can do.
