How Override works

Built for the moment an urge is already active.

Override creates immediate friction, redirects your attention, and helps you regain enough distance to make a deliberate choice.

The intervention

A guided interruption between the urge and the automatic route.

Start in the moment

Open Override as soon as you recognize the familiar pattern beginning.

Create temporary friction

Selected apps can be shielded during the intervention so the easiest route is no longer immediately available.

Complete an active reset

Override guides you through a structured reset designed to shift attention away from the urge and back toward deliberate action.

Return with more distance

When the intervention ends, you are no longer making the decision from the exact same mental state in which the urge began.

Set up while calm

Prepare before the high-risk moment arrives.

Choose the apps you want available for temporary shielding and personalize the parts of Override that support your reset. Once setup is complete, the intervention is ready when you need it.

Private by design

Sensitive recovery data stays on your device.

Override does not require an account or public profile. Core intervention data is stored locally, subject to the permissions and system controls available on your device.
