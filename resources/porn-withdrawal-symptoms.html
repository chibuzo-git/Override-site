Porn Withdrawal Symptoms

Some people report irritability, restlessness, strong urges, low mood, sleep changes, or concentration problems after stopping.

Override Recovery Library

Direct answer: Some people report irritability, restlessness, strong urges, low mood, sleep changes, or concentration problems after stopping.

Why this feels harder than it should

Discomfort can feel like proof that quitting made things worse.

The belief shift

Discomfort can be temporary without being meaningless.

What to do next

Track symptoms, protect sleep, move your body, reduce trigger exposure, and seek clinical help for severe or persistent symptoms.

Where Override fits

Override is built for the acute window. It can create temporary friction around selected apps and guide you into an active reset without publishing the full intervention method online.

It is not a replacement for therapy, accountability, community, or deeper recovery work. It is the tool you prepare for the moment those supports are hardest to access.
