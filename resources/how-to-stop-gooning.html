How to Stop Gooning

Break the extended stimulation loop with environmental friction, movement, and a pre-decided interruption sequence.

Override Recovery Library · Compulsive use

This guide focuses on practical steps you can prepare while calm and use when the pattern becomes difficult to interrupt.

Interrupt the extended stimulation loop

Gooning is sustained by repeated novelty, anticipation, and the decision to keep the session going. The earlier the interruption, the less momentum you have to overcome.

Close every continuation route

End tabs, move the device, leave the location, and block the apps or browsers that keep the loop alive. Partial closure often becomes a negotiation.

Change your physical state

Walk, shower, breathe slowly, or complete a demanding task. The goal is to pull attention out of passive stimulation and into a different sensory environment.

Do not chase a perfect final moment

The urge to stop only after one more clip or one more search is part of the loop. Ending imperfectly is still ending.

Address the setup later

After the moment passes, examine boredom, isolation, sleep, stress, and access. The acute interruption handles now; system changes reduce the next occurrence.

Where Override fits
