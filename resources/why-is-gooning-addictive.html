Why Does Gooning Feel Addictive?

Prolonged novelty, anticipation, and repeated stimulation can lock attention into a high-reward loop that is hard to interrupt.

Override Recovery Library

Direct answer: Prolonged novelty, anticipation, and repeated stimulation can lock attention into a high-reward loop that is hard to interrupt.

Why this feels harder than it should

The longer it continues, the less urgent real life feels.

The belief shift

The loop is built around continuation, not satisfaction.

What to do next

Reduce novelty access, interrupt earlier, avoid partial continuation, and seek help if sessions are escalating.
