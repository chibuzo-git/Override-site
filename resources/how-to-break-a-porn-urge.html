How to Break a Porn Urge

Use a concrete interruption sequence instead of negotiating with the urge while it is strongest.

Override Recovery Library · Urges

This guide focuses on practical steps you can prepare while calm and use when the pattern becomes difficult to interrupt.

Do not negotiate

Negotiation keeps attention attached to the urge. Replace the debate with a pre-decided action.

Break visual and physical continuity

Close the screen, stand up, leave the room, and change lighting or posture.

Create friction

Shield apps, block sites, hand the device to someone, or place it where retrieval takes effort.

Engage in a bounded task

Complete something measurable that occupies attention and movement.

Review only after the peak

Once calm returns, identify the cue and improve the setup. Analysis during the peak can become another form of delay.
