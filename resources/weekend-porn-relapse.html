Why Porn Relapse Happens on Weekends

Weekend relapse often rises because routine disappears and unstructured private time expands.

Override Recovery Library

Direct answer: Weekend relapse often rises because routine disappears and unstructured private time expands.

Why this feels harder than it should

The same person who feels stable during workdays can struggle as soon as Saturday becomes empty.

The belief shift

Unstructured time is not neutral when the old loop knows how to fill it.

What to do next

Plan the highest-risk blocks, leave the house, create social contact, and use activities with clear start and end points.

Where Override fits
