What to Do After a Porn Relapse

Stop the session, prevent one lapse from becoming a binge, identify the immediate trigger, and resume your plan now.

Why this feels harder than it should

The most dangerous thought is often “I already ruined everything, so it no longer matters.”

The belief shift

A lapse is information. The spiral is a second decision.

What to do next

End the behavior, change rooms, remove access, write down what happened immediately before, and make one concrete adjustment.

Where Override fits

Override is built for the acute window. It can create temporary friction around selected apps and guide you into an active reset without publishing the full intervention method online.

It is not a replacement for therapy, accountability, community, or deeper recovery work. It is the tool you prepare for the moment those supports are hardest to access.
