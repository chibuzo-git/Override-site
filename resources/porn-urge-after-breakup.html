Porn Urges After a Breakup

After a breakup, porn can become an immediate escape from loneliness, rejection, and emotional withdrawal.

Override Recovery Library

Direct answer: After a breakup, porn can become an immediate escape from loneliness, rejection, and emotional withdrawal.

Why this feels harder than it should

The relief is short, but the shame and isolation afterward can make the original pain worse.

The belief shift

Treat the heartbreak and the compulsive route as connected problems.

What to do next

Reduce private access during vulnerable hours, increase real human contact, and seek support for the loss.

Where Override fits

Override is built for the acute window. It can create temporary friction around selected apps and guide you into an active reset without publishing the full intervention method online.

It is not a replacement for therapy, accountability, community, or deeper recovery work. It is the tool you prepare for the moment those supports are hardest to access.
