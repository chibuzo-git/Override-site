Porn Addiction Help: Where to Start

A clear starting point for people who feel stuck, including environment changes, support, and immediate urge planning.

Override Recovery Library · Getting started

This guide focuses on practical steps you can prepare while calm and use when the pattern becomes difficult to interrupt.

Start without debating the label

You do not need to settle a diagnosis before responding to behavior that feels out of control or conflicts with your values.

Reduce immediate access

Remove saved material, change device routines, and add restrictions around the routes you use most often.

Identify the repeating conditions

Look for time, place, emotion, fatigue, boredom, conflict, and isolation. These conditions often matter more than general motivation.

Create an urge plan

Write one action for the first minute, one for the next ten minutes, and one person or professional resource to contact when the plan is not enough.

Seek qualified support

A therapist, clinician, or appropriate recovery group can help with compulsivity, trauma, anxiety, relationship strain, or other drivers that an app cannot resolve.

Where Override fits

Override is built for the active moment. It temporarily shields selected apps and guides you through an active guided reset. It is not a replacement for therapy or deeper recovery work.

Put the interruption in place before the urge.

Set up your shielded apps, missions, and personal phrase while you are calm.
