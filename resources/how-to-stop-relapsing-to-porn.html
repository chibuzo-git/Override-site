How to Stop Relapsing to Porn

Move from vague resolve to a specific relapse-prevention system built around triggers, access, and immediate action.

Override Recovery Library · Relapse

This guide focuses on practical steps you can prepare while calm and use when the pattern becomes difficult to interrupt.

Stop treating relapse as one mysterious event

Relapse usually follows a repeatable chain: trigger, opportunity, automatic route, behavior, and aftermath. Map the chain instead of only judging the outcome.

Track urges, not just failures

Record time, location, emotion, device, and what happened immediately before the urge. Patterns become visible when successful interruptions are tracked too.

Choose one response per trigger

Create an if-then rule for your highest-risk pattern. Example: “If I am alone in bed after midnight and the urge starts, I put the phone across the room and begin my reset sequence.”

Add friction before motivation disappears

Move devices, shield apps, remove saved access, and prepare the environment while calm. Do not rely on setting up protection during the urge.

Recover from a lapse immediately

A lapse becomes more dangerous when it is interpreted as total failure. Resume the plan in the next moment, identify the broken link, and adjust the system.
