How to Stop Masturbating to Porn

Separate triggers, reduce access, and build a response that works before the familiar sequence takes over.

Override Recovery Library · Behavior change

This guide focuses on practical steps you can prepare while calm and use when the pattern becomes difficult to interrupt.

Separate porn from masturbation in your analysis

Identify whether the main problem is compulsive porn use, masturbation, the combination, or the consequences around them. Clear definitions produce better plans.

Map the first step

The decisive moment may be opening social media, taking the phone to bed, or searching suggestive content—not the final behavior.

Remove the first-step cue

Change the device routine, location, or time window that starts the sequence. Earlier interventions require less force.

Prepare a replacement sequence

Choose a physical task, a contact, and a blocking action. A replacement should be specific and easy to begin.

Get support for persistent compulsivity

A therapist or qualified clinician can help when the behavior is frequent, distressing, escalating, or interfering with daily life.

Where Override fits

Override is built for the active moment. It temporarily shields selected apps and guides you through an active guided reset. It is not a replacement for therapy or deeper recovery work.
