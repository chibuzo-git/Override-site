Why Porn Blockers Alone Fail

Porn blockers are useful, but blocking alone does not address the active urge, the thought loop, or the route around the barrier.

Override Recovery Library · Blocking

Porn blockers do not fail because blocking is useless. They fail when blocking is expected to do the entire job.

Blocking still matters

A blocker can remove immediate access, interrupt cues, and make an automatic route harder to complete. That friction is useful.

The problem is the word alone

An active urge does not vanish simply because one route is closed. The person may search for another device, disable settings, switch apps, or continue feeding the thought loop.

A barrier needs a next action

The strongest setup pairs blocking with a predefined response: leave the room, contact someone, complete a task, or start an intervention. The blocker creates the gap; the action determines what fills it.

Blocking does not retrain the moment by itself

Long-term change requires repeatedly practicing a different response to the same cue. A barrier can prevent behavior, but an active competing routine helps build a new pattern.

Use blocking as one layer

Combine reliable friction, environmental design, active interruption, personal reasons, and deeper support. Blocking is infrastructure, not the entire recovery plan.
